# Payodhi — Maritime Oil Spill Attribution System (SIH-26143)

> **Tactical Maritime Defense & Intelligence Console for Automated Satellite SAR Oil Spill Detection, Hydrodynamic Drift Hindcasting, and Multi-Factor Suspect Vessel Attribution.**  
> Developed for **Smart India Hackathon (SIH-26143)** in partnership with **NTRO** (National Technical Research Organisation) & the **Indian Coast Guard (ICG)**.

## 🗺️ Key Capabilities

- **Tactical Map (Leaflet):** Real-time GIS visualization with basemap toggles, SAR slick polygons, hindcast drift paths, forward 24h forecast with uncertainty radii, and AIS vessel tracks.
- **Interactive Time Machine:** Scrub backwards through time to visually trace vessels and spill drift back to the moment of release.
- **5-Factor Attribution Engine:** Algorithmic ranking with radar charts evaluating drift agreement, time overlap, spatial proximity, vessel characteristics, and behavioral anomalies.
- **AI Maritime Copilot:** Forensic chat assistant answering operational and legal queries.
- **Print-Ready Intelligence Dossier:** Official 8-section legal/military classified dossier with multi-page print CSS.
- **SAR Image Ingestion Pipeline:** Drag-and-drop satellite imagery uploader with automated 6-phase forensic execution.
